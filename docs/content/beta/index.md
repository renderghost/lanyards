## 🔬 What's working now

- Bluesky Sign-in: Use your existing Bluesky account via OAuth

- Research Profile: Mobile-first design with QR code for easy sharing

- Professional Details: Add honorifics, location, and institutional affiliations

- Academic Networks: Link to ORCID, Google Scholar, ResearchGate, and more

- Publications: Add research works using DOIs (metadata fetched automatically)

- Event Tracking: Document your conference talks and symposium presentations

- Collections: Organise your links into themed groups
